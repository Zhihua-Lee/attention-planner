# v0.1.8 Adaptive day view, full width

## Changes

See the 0.1.8 changelog entry.

## Automated checks

- Unit: all 57 pass. New: the hour height is 48 px with nothing short, grows for a half-hour item, and stops at 120 px.
- Browser: 112 pass, 2 skipped on phone (the keyboard test, and full width, which phones do not need). New: three
  back-to-back events (12:30–13:30, 13:00–13:30, 13:30–14:20) make the hours taller, an hour block is twice a half-hour
  block, nothing covers the next block, and the half-hour block shows both its title and its time inside it; full
  width widens the app and is remembered after a reload.

## Manual checks

- Screenshots of that day on desktop and phone in dark mode: titles and times readable, side by side where they
  overlap.
- Live `/ics` on todo.onthat.top after deploying: a public calendar returned 200 with `BEGIN:VCALENDAR`, a web page
  returned 415, and a localhost address returned 400.

## Limits

- A day with a 15-minute item is shown at 120 px per hour (the most); shorter items show their title on one line.
