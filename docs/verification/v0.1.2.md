# v0.1.2 Outlook-like calendar

## Changes

NOW's agenda is replaced by a time grid with day, 3-day and week views, an all-day strip, side-by-side layout for
overlapping events, locations, a now line, and click-to-reserve. Wide screens get a two-column NOW. All-day events are
now kept from Outlook and .ics instead of being dropped.

## Automated checks

- Unit: all 43 pass. New: side-by-side layout of overlapping blocks (lanes and reuse); all-day events from the Outlook
  export and .ics.
- Browser: 71 pass, 1 skipped (desktop, phone, reduced motion). New: moving by day, three days and week; deadlines and
  untimed tasks in the all-day strip; overlapping events placed side by side; clicking an empty slot to reserve it.

## Manual checks

Screenshots with a realistic week (a repeating class with a location, overlapping meetings, a lunch inside a seminar,
an all-day holiday, reserved times, deadlines and untimed tasks): week at 1366 px; day, 3-day and week at 390 px; light
and dark. They showed the header columns out of line with the grid on phones and the first block's title hidden under
the header; both were fixed.

## Limits

- Events cannot be edited here; they come from Outlook (through the export) or an .ics file.
- Dragging to move or resize a reserved time is not built.
